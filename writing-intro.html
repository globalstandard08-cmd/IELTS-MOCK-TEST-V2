WRITING SECTION Total Time: 60 Minutes

Instructions to Candidates

About the Writing Test

  • The Writing test consists of two tasks.
  • You should spend about 20 minutes on Task 1 and about 40 minutes on Task 2.
  • Total time allowed: 60 minutes.
  • Answers must be written in full sentences; bullet points or notes are not acceptable.
  • Write your answers in the space provided.

Task Requirements

  • Writing Task 1: You will be presented with a graph, table, chart, diagram, or plan. You should summarise the information, describe the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.
  • Writing Task 2: You will be given a point of view, argument, or problem. Write an essay responding to the prompt, presenting a clear position and supporting it with reasons and examples. Write at least 250 words.
⏱️ Important: Once you click "Start Writing", the timer will begin and run continuously for 60 minutes. You can move between Task 1 and Task 2 freely, but the timer will not stop or reset. Answers are saved automatically as you type.

Test Conditions

  • You may use backspace, delete, cut, copy, and paste functions.
  • You can see your current word count displayed below the answer area.
  • Do not include your name, personal details, or irrelevant information in your answers.
  • When time is up, you will no longer be able to edit your work.
