Instructions for the Reading Test

The Reading test lasts 60 minutes. There are 40 questions in total.

What you need to know:

  • There are 3 reading passages of increasing difficulty.
  • All answers must be entered directly on the screen.
  • There is no extra time at the end to transfer answers.
  • You can change your answers any time before submitting.
  • Each correct answer receives one mark.

Task types may include: Multiple choice, True/False/Not Given, Yes/No/Not Given, matching headings, matching features, sentence completion, summary completion, and short answer questions.

Important: Once you click "Start Reading Test", the timer will begin immediately and cannot be paused. Please ensure you are ready before proceeding.
